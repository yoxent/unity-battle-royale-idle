# Maze of the Cursed Messiah 

*The Cursed Messiah is long gone, but people are still reluctant to come close to the maze. Now it is controlled by a party of orcs.*

1. A great stone double door on the eastern wall of the small chamber.
2. An uncanny, translucent comb locked in a  safe.
3. A rear entrance into the maze.
4. A giant pile of rubble hides lockpicks and a helm.
5. A iron throne, makes a person smaller when sat on.
6. A crate holds a golden figurine.
