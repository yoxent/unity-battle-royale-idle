# Twilight Maze of the Vampire Lady 

*The Vampire Lady is long gone, but people are still afraid to come close to the maze. Recently an undead wyvern has made its lair here. The maze of the Vampire Lady is home to a valuable specie of herbs.*

1. A large pile of rubble hides a torch.
2. A creepy doll, tells a story of the maze when picked up.
3. A knife of force in a glass trophy case.
4. A large double door with two keyholes on the eastern wall of the small chamber.
5. Gems in a battered chest.
6. A mutilated body of a human, an enchanted spear, some gold and a cloak close to it.
7. A rear entrance into the maze.
8. A dying dwarf, a brass key among his belongings.
9. A corpse of a thief, a brass key close by.
