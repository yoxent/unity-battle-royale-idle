# Plaguecrown Chambers 

*The chambers are situated deep in the dark woodland, in uncharted lands. Lately they were squatted by a party of cultists. Word is that Lech, a legendary knife, is still hidden here.*

1. A weird, translucent skull hovering in the middle of the chamber.
2. A halfling, sitting in a corner.
3. A rear entrance into the chambers. A dying gnome, a silver key among his belongings.
4. A huge door with a keyhole to the east.
5. A wall panel conceals a strange, unnaturally light box.
