# Citadel of Iron Moon 

*The citadel is situated high in the Uadjit-Dardai mountain peaks. These days it is badly infested by crows, which don't care about the history of the place.*

1. A scorched gate with two keyholes to the west.
2. A corpse of a gnoll, some gold nearby.
3. A mysterious, covered with hieroglyphs tome in the middle of a circle of runes on the ground.
4. A rear entrance into the citadel. A broken box holds a moon-shaped key.
5. A strongbox with a moon-shaped key.
6. A noble. Needs help to get out from the citadel.
7. A helm and a javelin hidden in a crevice of the floor.
8. A weird, made of an unknown material object in a battered locker.
